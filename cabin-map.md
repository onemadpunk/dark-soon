# Dark Soon: the whole cabin

60 rooms in 7 areas. Each area brings in its own new challenge, so the game keeps changing as you explore. A few areas are locked until you find an ability, the way Jet Set Willy's house had places you only reached by finding the right route, but gentler.

Roughly 100 fireflies in total (Jet Set Willy had 83 items).

## Abilities that open new areas

| Ability | Where you find it | What it does | What it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhona's Umbrella | Mudroom | Hold jump while falling to float down slowly, and ride the wind | The Roof |
| Slipper Boots | Linen Closet | Jump off walls | Chimney Climb, Coal Chute |
| Bean's Tongue | Lily Pond | Bean grabs hooks so Isaac can swing | Dusty Rafters, Rope Bridge |
| Seven Brass Keys | One in each area | Unlocks the Door With Seven Locks | The secret area and the finale |

## The sunset

With 60 rooms, 5 minutes is too short. In the full game the sun takes about 40 minutes to set, and each lantern you light pushes it back a little. It still never ends the game. After dark, the cabin's creatures get livelier until you finish.

## Progress

- **Batch 1 (done):** the ground floor, saving, the map, the Umbrella.
- **Batch 2 (done, 2026-10-11):** upstairs and the attic, 27 rooms and 157 fireflies so far. The rooms are now Manic Miner hard, at Steven's request:
  - Any bump sends Isaac back to the doorway they came in by, and the room starts over. Caught fireflies stay caught.
  - Falling more than 9 tiles hurts (Isaac looks scared just before it's too late).
  - Losing all three hearts sends Isaac back to the last lantern, and touching a lantern refills the hearts.
  - Fireflies sit over toy bricks, tacks and forks, behind patrols, on crumbling shelves and on trunk lids that open and shut.
  - The Slipper Boots (Linen Closet) give wall jumps. They are the only way up the Bathroom shaft to the Dark Attic, which has the third key.
- **Batch 3 (done, 2026-10-11):** the roof (y=1, 6 rooms) and the cellar (y=5, 9 rooms). 42 rooms, 274 fireflies and 8 golden fireflies so far.
  - New hazards and tricks: wind, slippery ice, sticky jam, steam vents, swimming in the Flooded Cellar, raindrops, crows, wasps, bats, fish and rolling barrels.
  - Friends who want things (Dizzy style): Corvin the crow swaps his key for the shiny button (Barrel Store), and Tilly the mouse opens her door for the freezer cheese.
  - Isaac Quest nod: the Boiler Room cupboard has a keypad. Its code 7251 shows only as an upside-down reflection in the Flooded Cellar water.
  - Rhona waits by the Door With Seven Locks and cheers Isaac on. The door counts the keys found.
  - Golden fireflies are optional extras in hard-to-reach corners.
- **Batch 4 (done, 2026-10-11):** the woods (x10 to 16) and the Dusty Rafters (9,2). 55 rooms, 382 fireflies and 13 golden fireflies.
  - Bean's Tongue is in the Lily Pond. In the air near a hook, press jump to swing; jump again to let go and fling. Needed for the Rope Bridge, Firefly Meadow, Dusty Rafters and the Crystal Cave's top.
  - New tricks: snapping plants, hedgehogs, falling logs, vines that grow and shrivel, lily pads that sink, mine carts on rails, dripping mine, crystals that light up when touched, runaway toilet rolls.
  - Gerald the raccoon won't leave the Outhouse until he gets a new comic (in the Treehouse). He swaps it for the sixth key. The seventh key is in the Dusty Rafters. All seven keys can now be found.
  - Ways in: the Front Porch's right door, the Balcony's right door (to the Outhouse), and the Window Nobody Opens' right door (to the Rafters).
  - The evening is now 35 minutes long to cover the extra rooms.
- Not built yet from the upstairs and attic lists: Spider Corner's sagging webs are plain web shelves for now.
- Reachability check: `node check-rooms.js` (reads index.html directly).

## How it gets built

In batches, each one playable at the same link before the next starts:

1. **Engine upgrade and the ground floor.** Rooms drawn as simple text maps so they're quick to design, progress saved in the browser (60 rooms is too long for one sitting), a map screen, and the Umbrella.
2. **Upstairs and the attic.** Rising water, falling shelves, dark rooms, Slipper Boots.
3. **The roof and the cellar.** Wind, steam, ice, swimming.
4. **The woods.** Bean's Tongue, sinking lily pads, mine carts.
5. **The secret area and the finale.**
